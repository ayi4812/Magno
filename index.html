01 — About

Artist statement

I'm still learning, still sketching and dreaming big. Every piece I make is a step toward the artist I want to become.

I'm a Multimedia Arts student who loves working across illustration, motion graphics, brand identity and interface design. My process starts with a simple sketch and a lot of curiosity, then grows into something intentional, from first draft to final export.

I'm in my second year, building strong foundations in drawing, digital imaging and animation. I dream of a career in visual storytelling, and I'm looking for chances to collaborate, learn from working artists and keep growing, one project at a time.

  • Digital illustration
  • Motion & 2D animation
  • Brand & poster design
  • UI / interface design

01 — About

Artist
statement

I believe good design is quiet confidence: clear ideas, honest colour and a sense of play. My work sits where art meets technology, using digital tools to tell stories people actually remember.

As a Multimedia Arts student, I’m learning to move comfortably between disciplines, from sketching characters and composing posters to editing short films and building brand identities. Every project is a chance to ask better questions and to leave the viewer feeling something.
